# Widget Inspection Guidance (Region B)
Version 2.0 | Effective: 2026-03-01

> Synthetic example guidance for the DecisionLedger example reasoner. It
> describes imaginary widgets and carries no real policy content.

## 1. Scope

EX-WIDGET-GUIDE-B offers guidance for widgets inspected in REGION_B. Unlike
REGION_A, REGION_B does not require calibration review for widgets with a
thin inspection history; it recommends a sprocket inspection instead, which
checks gizmo alignment and records the outcome next to the widget score.

## 2. Sprocket Inspection

A sprocket inspection SHOULD be performed on any widget whose widget score is
above 0.30. The inspector verifies gizmo alignment, confirms the widget
identifier matches the ledger entry, and notes any deviation from the expected
widget profile. A widget whose sprocket inspection passes MAY be released with
the inspection recorded as a required control.

## 3. Holding a Widget

A widget whose wobble exceeds 0.60 SHOULD be held for a widget steward when
the sprocket inspection cannot be completed the same day. Holding is
preferred to blocking in REGION_B: most wobbling widgets are re-seated rather
than discarded.
