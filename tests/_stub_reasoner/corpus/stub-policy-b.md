# Stub Widget Guidance B
Version 2.0 | Effective: 2024-03-01

> This document is synthetic test data for the DecisionLedger framework test
> suite. It describes imaginary widgets and carries no real policy content.

## 1. Scope

STUB-POLICY-B offers non-binding guidance for widgets observed in REGION_B.
Unlike REGION_A, REGION_B does not require calibration review for widgets with
a thin history; instead it recommends a lighter sprocket inspection that checks
the widget's gizmo alignment and records the inspection outcome alongside the
widget score in the regional widget ledger.

## 2. Sprocket Inspection

A sprocket inspection SHOULD be performed on any widget whose score exceeds the
regional attention threshold. The inspector verifies gizmo alignment, confirms
the widget identifier matches the ledger entry, and notes any deviation from
the expected widget profile. Inspection findings are advisory and do not by
themselves block a release decision made under REGION_B guidance.

## 3. Cross-Region Conflicts

When a widget is subject to both REGION_A policy and REGION_B guidance, the
stricter REGION_A calibration review requirement applies and the decision
SHOULD be held for a widget steward. Reasoners citing this section SHALL also
cite the corresponding REGION_A section so that an auditor can see the conflict
that caused the hold and verify how it was resolved.
