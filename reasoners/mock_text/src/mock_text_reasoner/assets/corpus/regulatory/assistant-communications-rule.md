# Automated Assistant Communications Rule
Version 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

> Synthetic regulatory text for the DecisionLedger text mock. It governs an
> imaginary bank's automated assistant and carries no real legal content.

## 1. Scope

MT-REG-ASSISTANT governs every message a customer sends to the bank's
automated assistant, in chat, email, or the app. The assistant may reply to
a message, or act on it, without a person reviewing it first only where this
rule permits it; every other message waits for a person.

## 2. General Questions

The assistant MAY answer a general question about the bank's services
without review: opening hours, published fees, interest rates, statements,
and branch locations. Such an answer repeats published information and
commits the customer to nothing, so an automated reply is permitted.

## 3. Changes to Contact Details

A request to change the customer's address, phone number, or email address
SHALL be confirmed with a step-up identity check before the change is made.
The assistant SHALL NOT change contact details on the strength of the message
alone; after the check succeeds, the change may proceed.

## 4. Statements of Financial Hardship

A message in which a customer states financial hardship (a lost job, being
unable to pay, falling behind on payments) SHALL be held for review by a
trained agent before any reply is sent. The assistant SHALL NOT send an
automated reply to such a message, including an automated hardship-assistance
reply: a trained agent reads the message first and answers it.
