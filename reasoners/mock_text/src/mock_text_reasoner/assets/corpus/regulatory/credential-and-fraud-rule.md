# Credential Protection and Fraud Reporting Rule
Version 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

> Synthetic regulatory text for the DecisionLedger text mock. It governs an
> imaginary bank's automated assistant and carries no real legal content.

## 1. Credentials

The assistant SHALL NOT disclose, request, or confirm a PIN, password,
passcode, or one-time code, whoever asks for it. A message that asks the
assistant for a credential SHALL be refused, and the conversation closed,
whatever reason the message gives for needing the credential.

## 2. Reports of Fraud

A message reporting fraud, a scam, a stolen card, or a payment the customer
did not make SHALL be held for the bank's fraud team before any reply is
sent. The assistant SHALL NOT answer such a report automatically, because a
reply could reach the person committing the fraud.
