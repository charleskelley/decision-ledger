# Escalation Practice
Version 1.0 | Effective: 2026-03-01

> Synthetic practice note for the DecisionLedger text mock. It describes an
> imaginary bank's assistant team and carries no real policy content.

## 1. Fraud Reports

A message that reports fraud, a scam, a stolen card, or a payment the
customer did not make goes to the fraud team's queue and is held there; the
assistant sends no automated reply, and a fraud analyst contacts the customer
through the phone number on file.

## 2. Credential Requests

When a message asks the assistant for a PIN, password, passcode, or one-time
code, the assistant refuses and closes the conversation. It never reads out,
sends, or confirms a credential, and it does not offer to make an exception
for a customer who says it is urgent.

## 3. Messages the Assistant Cannot Place

A message the assistant cannot place in a known category is held for an
agent, who reads it and answers it. The assistant does not guess at what an
unfamiliar message asks for, because a wrong automated answer is worse than a
short wait.
